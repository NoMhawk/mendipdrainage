## Other trades and services we rely on
- **Tanker / desludging firm** (registered waste carrier) for emptying the old tank before removal.
- **Electrician** (Part P registered) for the plant's power supply and controls.
- **Plant hire** (360 excavator, dumper) if not owned.
- **Building control:** Somerset Council, Dorset Council, or a registered building control approver.
- **Percolation testing:** done in house to Approved Document H 1.34–1.38.

## What changes the price
1. Size of plant (number of bedrooms → population equivalent).
2. Where the treated water goes: existing outfall to a flowing stream (cheapest), new drainage field (more digging, stone, pipe), or pumped discharge.
3. Ground: rock, high water table, clay (poor percolation), access for the digger.
4. Distance from the house and power supply run.
5. Removing or filling the old tank.
6. Reinstating lawns, drives and paths.
7. Building control fee.
