**Copy rule:** town pages describe the catchment as "likely" (or "not certain") and tell the reader to check with the council. Catchment boundaries do not follow town edges, and individual properties can fall either side. Somerset Council says the Somerset Levels and Moors catchment covers the Brue, Parrett and Tone; the north side of the Mendips (Midsomer Norton, Chew Valley, Bath, Frome) drains to the Bristol Avon instead. Whether the Somerset River Axe area (Cheddar, Wedmore, Weston) and the Burnham coast count is not confirmed: those pages say so.

Dropped when the base moved (now more than about an hour from Wells): Beaminster, Ilminster, Chard, Sturminster Newton, Dorchester, Bridport, Shaftesbury, Axminster, Lyme Regis, Blandford Forum, Honiton, Wellington.
