## Patterns across competitors
1. Phone first. Every site has the phone number at the top. Forms come second.
2. "Free quote" or "free site visit" is the standard offer.
3. **Nobody publishes prices.** This is the biggest gap.
4. Trust is mostly Trustpilot and Google stars. Only Allerton shows British Water accreditation.
5. Guarantee terms are rare and vague.
6. Regulation content (general binding rules, selling a house) is used to win leads.
7. Only Homeseptic does town pages, and they are thin.

## Gaps we will use
1. **Real local pages** for Yeovil and every town within an hour, each with local detail (council, river catchment, phosphate status, distance from base).
2. **Published price guides**, with honest ranges and what changes the price.
3. **Selling-a-house focus.** A fast "sale-ready" route for sellers, buyers, estate agents and conveyancers: survey in days, written compliance pack, fixed quote.
4. **Honest phosphate advice.** We tell people if they may qualify for a funded upgrade elsewhere. Builds trust in an area where WCI owns the "free" message.
5. **Clear guarantee and aftercare** in writing (Rich to decide the terms).
6. **British Water accreditation** for servicing engineers (Rich to get).
7. **One job, one firm.** Survey, design, building control, install, electrics, paperwork and first service, so the customer deals with one firm.
