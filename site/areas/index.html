Yeovil and about an hour around.

We are based in Yeovil and cover towns and villages within about an hour's drive. If you are not sure whether we cover you, just ask.

Each town page explains the local picture: which council handles building control, which river the area drains to, and whether phosphate or other nutrient rules are likely to apply. That matters because the right system for a cottage on the Somerset Levels is often very different from one on the Dorset chalk or the Mendip limestone.
