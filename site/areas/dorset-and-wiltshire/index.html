Septic tank replacement in Dorset and Wiltshire

Towns and villages we cover in Dorset and Wiltshire, within about an hour of Wells.

North Dorset around Sherborne and Gillingham, and west Wiltshire around Mere and Warminster, are within an hour of our Wells base. Both areas have a lot of isolated farms and cottages a long way from any sewer. Sherborne sits on the River Yeo, which drains to the Somerset Levels, while Warminster is on the Wylye, part of the Hampshire Avon system with its own nutrient rules for new development. The general binding rules for existing septic tanks and treatment plants are the same here as anywhere in England.
