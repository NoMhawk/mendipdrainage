We work across Wellington and nearby villages including Sampford Arundel, Rockwell Green, Langford Budville, West Buckland, Nynehead, Wrangway.

Local rules: River Tone and the catchment

Wellington is on the River Tone, which we understand drains to the Somerset Levels and Moors, an internationally protected wetland (Ramsar site). That matters in two ways:

  • New homes in the catchment have to be "nutrient neutral" for phosphorus, which can affect the drainage design for self-builds and conversions.
  • Existing homes may be able to get an upgrade funded through a phosphate credit scheme. How the Somerset schemes work.

Catchment lines don't follow town boundaries, so check your exact property with Somerset Council.

Building regulations approval for a new treatment plant or septic tank goes through Somerset Council building control or a registered building control approver. We prepare and submit the application as part of the job.

Common questions

Do I need to replace my septic tank when selling a house in Wellington?

If it discharges to a stream, river or ditch, it needs upgrading, and the buyer and seller should agree who will do the work as a condition of sale. If it goes to a working drainage field, it may be fine. A survey will tell you.

Is Wellington in the Somerset phosphate area?

We understand Wellington drains to the Somerset Levels and Moors, so phosphate rules and credit schemes may be relevant. Check your exact property with Somerset Council.
