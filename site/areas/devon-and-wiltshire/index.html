Septic tank replacement in Devon and Wiltshire

Towns and villages we cover in Devon and Wiltshire, within about an hour of Yeovil.

East Devon around Axminster and Honiton, and south-west Wiltshire around Mere and Warminster, are within an hour of our Yeovil base. Both areas have a lot of isolated farms and cottages a long way from any sewer. The River Axe in Devon and the Hampshire Avon system in Wiltshire (which includes the Wylye at Warminster) have their own nutrient rules for new development, so new homes and conversions need careful drainage design. The general binding rules for existing septic tanks and treatment plants are the same here as anywhere in England.
