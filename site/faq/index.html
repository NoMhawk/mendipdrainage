Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we are asked most.

All questions

Is it illegal to have a septic tank?

No. Septic tanks are still allowed in England if they meet the general binding rules, which means discharging to ground through a proper drainage field, not to a stream, river or ditch.

Do I have to replace my septic tank when I sell my house?

If it discharges to a watercourse, it is already non-compliant and must be upgraded. GOV.UK says the buyer and seller should agree who will do the work, as a condition of sale. It does not have to be the seller, but it does have to be agreed.

How long does a replacement take?

Most domestic jobs take 3 to 5 working days on site. Building control approval and booking add time before that, so allow a few weeks from first visit to finish.

Will my garden be dug up?

Part of it, yes. We plan the route to keep disruption down, protect lawns and drives where we can, and reinstate the ground when we finish. Your quote says exactly what reinstatement is included.

Do I need planning permission?

Building regulations approval is always needed for a new septic tank or treatment plant. Planning permission is sometimes needed too. We check this for your property during the survey.

How much electricity does a treatment plant use?

It depends on the make and process. Most domestic plants run an air pump or motor all the time, so ask for the running cost of each option. We include this when we compare plants for you.

What happens in a power cut?

Approved Document H says a plant that needs power should be able to work without it for up to 6 hours, or have a backup supply. Plants still hold the waste in a power cut. Tell us if your area has long outages and we will factor it in.

Do you service treatment plants?

No. We install and replace systems. After handover, your plant needs a yearly service by a competent engineer, for example a British Water accredited service engineer. We install, we do not service, but the plant maker can recommend someone local. Your handover pack says what the maker requires.

How often does a treatment plant need emptying?

The general binding rules say at least once a year, or as the manufacturer says. See our guide on how often to empty a treatment plant.

Can I install a treatment plant myself?

It is possible but not something we would recommend. It needs building regulations approval, correct sizing, safe deep excavation, and electrical work by a registered electrician.

Is a conversion unit cheaper than a new plant?

Often the unit is cheaper and there is less digging. But if the tank is in poor condition or the unit does not meet the rules for your discharge, it can cost more in the end.

Will a buyer accept a converted tank?

Buyers and their solicitors want proof the system meets the rules. Certified equipment, building control records and a service history help. Without them, expect questions.

Who pays to replace a septic tank when a house is sold?

There is no fixed rule. GOV.UK says the buyer and seller should agree who is responsible, as a condition of sale. Often the seller pays or the price is reduced by the cost of the work.

Can I sell a house with a non-compliant septic tank?

Yes, but the buyer must be told and the upgrade has to be agreed as part of the sale. Expect the buyer to ask for a quote and a price reduction, or for the work to be done before completion.

Is there a septic tank compliance certificate?

There is no single official certificate. What buyers look for is evidence: building regulations approval, product certification (such as BS EN 12566), service and emptying records, and the written description the rules require. A survey report brings this together.

How long does it take?

A survey can usually be booked within days. Replacement takes 3 to 5 days on site once building control is in place. Tell us your completion date and we will say honestly whether it can be done in time.

Does a normal house survey cover the septic tank?

Usually not in any detail. A building survey or homebuyer report will note there is private drainage, but it will not normally tell you where it discharges or whether it meets the rules.

Should the buyer or seller pay for the survey?

Either. Buyers often pay for their own, but sellers who get one done before listing can answer questions quickly and keep a sale on track.

How big does a drainage field need to be?

It depends on the soil and the number of people. Approved Document H gives a formula based on the percolation result. A typical house field is tens of square metres of trenches.

Can I drive over a drainage field?

No. Approved Document H says no access roads, driveways or paved areas should be in the disposal area.

Why was a cesspit fitted in the first place?

Usually because there was no suitable outlet: no watercourse, poor ground, or rules at the time. Check this first, because the same limits may still apply.

Do I need a permit for a new treatment plant?

Not if you meet the general binding rules. You need a permit if you cannot meet them, for example because of volume, protected sites or a groundwater Source Protection Zone 1, and connecting to the sewer is not reasonable.

Is a free septic tank upgrade really free?

Under the schemes we have seen, the provider pays for the upgrade because they sell the phosphate credits it creates. Read the terms carefully, including who owns and maintains the new plant and any legal agreement on your property.

Does this apply in Dorset?

Parts of north Dorset, such as around Sherborne, drain to the Somerset Levels and Moors. Other parts of Dorset are covered by different nutrient rules, such as Poole Harbour. Check with Dorset Council.

Do I need to register my septic tank?

Not in England, if it meets the general binding rules. Registration was dropped when the rules came in. Wales and Scotland are different.

What happens if I ignore the rules?

The Environment Agency can take action if a system causes pollution or does not meet the rules. The bigger practical problem for most people is a sale falling through or being delayed.

Is a treatment plant better than a septic tank?

It cleans the water better and gives you more choice about where it goes. But it costs more to buy and run. If your ground drains well, a septic tank and drainage field is still a good, legal option.

My septic tank has gone to the stream for 50 years. Does it still need changing?

Yes. Age does not make it compliant. The old deadline of 1 January 2020 has passed.

Will I get a mortgage on a house with a septic tank?

Usually yes. Lenders may ask questions about a non-compliant system, so have the answers ready.
