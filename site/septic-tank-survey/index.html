Septic tank surveys

A clear written report on your septic tank, treatment plant or cesspit: what it is, where it goes, its condition, and whether it meets the general binding rules. For buyers, sellers and anyone who wants peace of mind.

What we check

  • Type of system: septic tank, treatment plant or cesspit, and the make and model if known
  • Size compared with the number of bedrooms
  • Condition of the tank, lids, baffles and pipes (with the tank emptied where needed)
  • Where it discharges: drainage field, soakaway, stream or ditch
  • Signs of failure: smells, ponding, lush or boggy ground, backing up
  • Rough distance to the nearest public sewer, and nearby protected sites
  • Paperwork: building regulations approval, service and emptying records

What you get

A plain-English report with photos, a sketch plan of where everything is, our view on whether it meets the general binding rules, and what (if anything) needs doing. If work is needed, we can give you a fixed quote.

When to get one

  • Before you list a house for sale. No surprises when the buyer's solicitor asks.
  • Before you exchange on a purchase. Know the cost before you commit.
  • If you think something is wrong. Smells, slow drains, wet ground.

CCTV and emptying

Some problems can only be seen inside the pipes or with the tank empty. If we think a CCTV drain survey or emptying is needed, we will tell you the extra cost before we do it.

Common questions

Does a normal house survey cover the septic tank?

Usually not in any detail. A building survey or homebuyer report will note there is private drainage, but it will not normally tell you where it discharges or whether it meets the rules.

Should the buyer or seller pay for the survey?

Either. Buyers often pay for their own, but sellers who get one done before listing can answer questions quickly and keep a sale on track.
