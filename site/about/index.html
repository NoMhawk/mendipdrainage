About us

A local team that does one job well: replacing old septic tanks with systems that meet today's rules.

Who we are

Mendip Drainage is a Wells-based firm that specialises in replacing old septic tanks with modern sewage treatment plants and drainage fields, across the Mendips, Somerset and the edges of Dorset and Wiltshire.

How we work

  • Honest first. If you can connect to the sewer, keep your existing system, or get a funded upgrade, we will say so.
  • Fixed written quotes. After a free site visit.
  • Done properly. Designed to the general binding rules, British Standards and Approved Document H, with building control sign-off.
  • Tidy. We protect your garden as far as we can and reinstate it when we finish.
  • Paperwork. A handover pack with everything you need to run the system and to sell your home one day.

Why "Mendip"?

Wells sits right under the Mendip Hills. Rain soaks into the limestone and comes back out as the springs that gave the city its name, and as the rivers that flow from the hills out across the Levels. Our job is getting clean water back into that system, so the name felt right for a Wells firm.
