The 2020 septic tank rules: what changed

You may have heard that septic tanks "had to be replaced by 2020". Here is what actually changed, and why it still matters.

What the deadline was

Under the general binding rules, septic tanks discharging directly to a watercourse had to be replaced or upgraded by 1 January 2020, or sooner if the property was sold before then. It applied to septic tanks going to surface water, not to those going to a proper drainage field.

What now

The deadline has passed, but the rule hasn't gone away. Any septic tank still discharging to a stream, river or ditch is non-compliant and needs upgrading as soon as possible. The current rules took effect on 2 October 2023.

Why sales bring it up

Buyers' solicitors ask where the tank discharges. If the answer is a watercourse, the buyer and seller need to agree who will do the work. See selling a house with a septic tank.
