Choosing a sewage treatment plant

There is no single best plant. The right one depends on your site, budget and how much you want to spend running it. Here is what to compare.

What to compare

  • Certification: BS EN 12566-3, and ideally on British Water's approved equipment list.
  • Size: sized to British Water Flows and Loads for your bedrooms.
  • Running cost: electricity used by the blower or motor.
  • Servicing: parts availability, service interval, and who locally can service it.
  • Depth and shape: deep or shallow dig, which matters on rock or a high water table.
  • Warranty: on the tank and the mechanical parts.

Common UK makes

Makes we commonly see and can fit include Kingspan Klargester (BioDisc, BioFicient), Marsh (Ensign, Ultra), Tricel (Novo), Graf (One2Clean), WPL (Diamond), Harlequin and Premier Tech (Ecoflo).

Online prices for a house-sized plant on its own were roughly £2,000 to £5,000 when we last checked (November 2026), before delivery and installation.

Our approach

We are not tied to one brand. We recommend what suits the site and explain why, with the running costs side by side. See treatment plant installation.
