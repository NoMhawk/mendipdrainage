Signs your septic tank is failing

Septic tanks can work quietly for decades, then fail. Catch the signs early and the fix is usually cheaper.

The warning signs

  1. Slow sinks, baths and toilets all over the house, not just one.
  2. Gurgling in the pipes.
  3. Sewage smells in the garden or near the tank.
  4. Wet, boggy or extra-green grass over the drainage area.
  5. Sewage backing up into the house or the first manhole.
  6. The tank fills quickly after it has been emptied.
  7. Dirty water where the pipe meets a ditch or stream.

What to do

First, get the tank emptied by a registered waste carrier and ask them what they saw. If problems come back quickly, the drainage field or tank has probably failed. Book a survey to find the cause before spending money.

Legal duty

Under the general binding rules you must repair or replace a system that is not in good working order, for example leaks, cracks, blockages, poor drainage or smells.
