Building regulations for septic tanks and treatment plants

Installing a new septic tank or treatment plant needs building regulations approval. Approved Document H, part H2, sets out how it should be done.

You need approval

GOV.UK says you need building regulations approval to install a new septic tank or small sewage treatment plant, and you may need planning permission too. If your current system was never approved, you may need to apply when you change it.

Key distances from Approved Document H

WhatDistance
Septic tank from habitable parts of buildingsAt least 7m
Septic tank from tanker accessWithin 30m
Treatment plant discharge from watercourses and other buildingsAt least 10m
Drainage field from any watercourse or permeable drainAt least 10m
Drainage field from any buildingAt least 15m
Drainage field from a groundwater supply abstractionAt least 50m

Other requirements

  • Plants should be type-tested, and if they need power, cope without it for up to 6 hours or have a backup supply.
  • A notice should be fixed inside the building describing the maintenance needed.
  • No driveways, paving or other services in the drainage field area.
