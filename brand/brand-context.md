## What the business is
A local firm, based in Yeovil, Somerset, that **replaces old septic tanks with modern sewage treatment plants**. Also: drainage fields, cesspit replacement, surveys for house sales, off-mains drainage for new builds, and servicing plans.

Why people need it: under the Environment Agency's general binding rules (England), a septic tank must not discharge to a stream, river or ditch. Those that do must be replaced or upgraded. A house sale is the usual trigger, because the buyer's survey or solicitor flags it. New builds off the mains also need a compliant system for building control. Full detail: `research/regulations.md`.

## Where
Yeovil and everywhere within about an hour's drive: south Somerset, north and west Dorset, east Devon, south-west Wiltshire. Town list: `research/service-area.md`.
